How Verification Works

Trust is the foundation of Crowd-Funding Egypt. We manually verify every project to ensure authenticity and impact.

Our Process

  1. Identity Check: We verify the identity of the project creator using official national ID documents.
  2. Project Review: Our team reviews the project description, goals, and feasibility.
  3. Fund Destination: We ensure that funds are directed to a valid, registered Egyptian bank account or e-wallet.
