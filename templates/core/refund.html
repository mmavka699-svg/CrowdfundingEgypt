Refund Policy

We want you to donate with peace of mind. Here is how our refunds work.

General Rules

Refunds are available under the following conditions:

  • If a project fails verification after being published.
  • If a project creator explicitly cancels their campaign before funds are disbursed.
  • If we suspect fraudulent activity related to the campaign.
Note: Once funds are successfully disbursed to the verified creator at the end of a successful campaign, refunds can no longer be processed by the platform.
